# TidySurf Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 15, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

TidySurf is a browser extension designed to block advertisements and trackers, improve browsing privacy, and give users control over their filtering preferences.

This Privacy Policy explains what information TidySurf handles, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Our approach is privacy-first: filtering preferences and website exceptions are stored locally on your device. Optional product analytics are separate from the core blocking functionality and require your explicit consent.

## 2. Information TidySurf Handles

### Information stored locally

TidySurf may store the following information on your device:

- Ad-blocking and protection preferences.
- Filter-list selections and settings.
- Website allowlist and exception preferences.
- Custom filtering rules and preferences.
- Other extension configuration settings.

This information is used to operate the extension and is stored locally using browser extension storage.

TidySurf does not send this local configuration information to our servers for advertising or profiling.

### Optional product analytics

TidySurf may offer optional product analytics through PostHog, our analytics service provider.

Analytics will be disabled by default. Data will only be transmitted after you explicitly opt in. Declining analytics or withdrawing consent will not affect TidySurf's core ad-blocking functionality.

If you opt in, the information collected may include limited events such as:

- Extension feature usage.
- Whether protection settings are enabled or disabled.
- Filter update success or failure.
- Technical error categories.
- General extension interaction events.

Analytics events are designed to contain only limited product usage information. They will not include:

- Visited website URLs or domains.
- Browsing history.
- Website or page content.
- Page titles.
- Search queries.
- Blocked request URLs.
- Contents of custom filters.
- Names, email addresses, or account credentials.

A randomly generated installation identifier may be used to understand product usage over time after consent is granted.

### IP addresses and network information

When optional analytics events are transmitted to PostHog, PostHog may receive your IP address as part of the network request.

We intend to configure PostHog to discard client IP addresses from stored event records and disable GeoIP enrichment. Discarding IP addresses from stored records does not mean the IP address is never received or processed during transmission.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information handled by TidySurf is used for the following purposes:

- Providing ad-blocking and tracker-blocking functionality.
- Remembering your extension preferences.
- Managing filter lists and website exceptions.
- Understanding optional product usage.
- Identifying technical problems.
- Improving extension reliability, performance, and functionality.

We do not use this information to serve targeted advertisements, sell user profiles, or build browsing-history profiles.

## 4. Third-Party Services

### PostHog

If you explicitly enable optional analytics, limited product interaction and technical events may be sent to PostHog.

PostHog processes this information on our behalf to help us understand product usage and improve TidySurf.

PostHog's privacy information is available at:

https://posthog.com/privacy

Analytics is optional and is not required to use the extension's core functionality.

## 5. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell user data.

We do not share user information with third parties for advertising, profiling, or unrelated commercial purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers, such as PostHog, only to support the purposes described in this policy.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Extension settings and filtering preferences remain stored locally until they are changed or removed by the user, or until the extension's local data is cleared or the extension is uninstalled.

Optional analytics data may be retained by PostHog according to the configured project retention settings and applicable service policies.

You can withdraw analytics consent through the extension's settings. Upon withdrawal, TidySurf will stop sending future analytics events and clear any locally queued analytics data and analytics identifier maintained by the extension.

## 7. Your Choices

You can:

- Use TidySurf without enabling optional analytics.
- Decline analytics consent.
- Withdraw analytics consent later through the extension settings.
- Change or remove filtering preferences and website exceptions.
- Clear extension data or uninstall TidySurf.

Declining or withdrawing analytics consent does not disable ad blocking or other core extension features.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the information handled by TidySurf and to protect information transmitted to our service providers.

Analytics events are designed around a restricted set of event names and properties to avoid collecting browsing activity or website-specific information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TidySurf's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

If a future change introduces additional data collection or materially changes how information is used, we will update this policy and provide any disclosures or consent mechanisms required by applicable platform policies.

The latest version will always be available at this URL.
